France is a country in Western Europe. Its capital is Paris, which is known for the Eiffel Tower, the Louvre Museum, and Notre-Dame Cathedral. The official language is French, and the currency is the euro.

Japan is an island country in East Asia. Its capital is Tokyo, one of the most populous cities in the world. Mount Fuji, at 3,776 meters, is the country's highest peak. The currency is the yen.

Brazil is the largest country in South America. Its capital is Brasília, though Rio de Janeiro and São Paulo are better known internationally. The official language is Portuguese, and the Amazon rainforest covers a large part of its territory.
